Best Figma Frame Settings for LinkedIn Carousels

To ensure your typography and UI elements stay crisp on retina displays and mobile screens, configure your Figma canvas properly before exporting:

  • Recommended Frame Size: 1080 x 1350 px (4:5 Aspect Ratio for maximum mobile feed real estate).
  • Alternative Frame Size: 1080 x 1080 px (1:1 Square).
  • Export Multiplier: Export frames at 1x or 2x PNG to retain sharp lines without generating massive multi-megabyte files.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why not use Figma's built-in "Export to PDF"?

Figma's built-in PDF export often results in huge file sizes, improperly ordered multi-page sequences, and inconsistent raster downsampling that affects mobile rendering on LinkedIn.

Are my Figma design files kept private?

Yes, CarouselCraft runs 100% client-side in your web browser. Your images and generated PDF never touch a remote server.
