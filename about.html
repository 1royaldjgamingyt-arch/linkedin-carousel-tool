About CarouselCraft

CarouselCraft is a free, web-based tool created to streamline the LinkedIn carousel publishing workflow for creators, marketers, and developers worldwide.

Why We Built This

Most existing carousel generators force users to sign up, subscribe to expensive plans, or degrade image quality during PDF conversion. CarouselCraft was built to provide a fast, privacy-first, zero-friction alternative that runs completely within your web browser.

Core Principles

  • Privacy First: No files are stored or transmitted across remote servers.
  • High Fidelity: Generates uncompressed 4:5 mobile-ready PDFs.
  • Free Accessibility: No paywalls, forced logins, or intrusive watermarks.
