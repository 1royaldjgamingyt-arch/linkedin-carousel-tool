Why 4:5 Portrait Ratio Dominates LinkedIn

Over 80% of LinkedIn interactions happen on mobile devices. A 4:5 aspect ratio (1080x1350px) occupies up to 30% more vertical screen space than a traditional square graphic, forcing the reader to pause their scroll and focus entirely on your carousel content.
