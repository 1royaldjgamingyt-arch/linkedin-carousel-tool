Frequently Asked Questions

Is this LinkedIn Carousel Tool free?

Yes. You can upload images and create a PDF in your browser without signing up. Your device and browser performance may affect how many large images you can process at once.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

This page reads the selected image files locally in your browser and builds the PDF on your device. The files are not sent to this website by the carousel tool.

What image dimensions should I use?

A 4:5 portrait image such as 1080 × 1350 pixels is a common carousel design size. Other image dimensions can also be exported; the PDF page will use a consistent portrait layout.

Can I change the slide order?

Yes. Drag the thumbnails to reorder the slides. The PDF is generated in the order shown in the slide deck.

Will the PDF contain a watermark?

The PDF export created by this tool does not add a watermark. Make sure you have permission to use the images included in your document.

Does this tool post directly to LinkedIn?

No. It creates a PDF file for you to review and upload to LinkedIn yourself.
