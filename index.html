Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is the best aspect ratio for LinkedIn carousels?

The ideal ratio is 4:5 Portrait (1080x1350 pixels). This dimensions cover maximum vertical space on mobile feeds, driving higher click-through rates and readability compared to standard square formats.

Are my uploaded images stored on your servers?

No. All PDF generation and previewing logic execute locally within your web browser using client-side JavaScript. None of your data or files are transmitted to or stored on external servers.

Is there a limit on how many slides I can export?

There is no hard limit on CarouselCraft. However, LinkedIn recommends keeping carousel decks between 4 to 12 slides for optimal reader retention.

Does the PDF export include any watermarks?

No. Every exported document is 100% clean and free of watermarks or branding, making it suitable for personal branding and enterprise use.
